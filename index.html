19:40 · Blue hour · The story

Music as a way back.

MUFASA is the Soul Trance project of Israeli producer and DJ Ofir Meir. Every track starts with a feeling or a message, and the music becomes its voice.

  1. Before

    Years of playing for fun

    An amateur musician for years. Music was something to enjoy on a night out, nothing more.

  2. The break

    Injury, then recovery

    A personal journey through injury and a long recovery. Somewhere in the middle of it, electronic music stopped being entertainment and became a tool for healing.

  3. Nine months

    The first note

    He opened the production software, wrote one note, and kept going. Nine months later the first track was ready.

  4. 13 Jul 2026

    Sound Healing is released

    A track that leads from pain and rage to light and calm, made with Limor Bachar on vocals and Ami Sterling on guitar.

  5. Now

    Journeys through sound, rhythm and emotion

    Original tracks, the Horizon Series of DJ sets, and long nights where his own music sits beside the trance artists he loves.
