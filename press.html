Long bio

MUFASA is the Soul Trance project of Israeli producer and DJ Ofir Meir.

After years as an amateur musician, a personal journey through injury and recovery led him to discover electronic music as more than entertainment. It became a tool for healing, and that experience now shapes every track he creates.

Blending progressive and psytrance with emotional storytelling, MUFASA starts each piece with a feeling or a message and lets the music become its voice. His sets, including the Horizon Series that follows a single night from sunset to dawn, mix his own tracks with music from the trance artists he loves.
